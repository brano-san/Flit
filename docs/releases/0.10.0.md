## New

- On Linux, the Network view lists nearby computers and services (SMB, SFTP, WebDAV, NFS) found through GVfs and Avahi, including Windows computers in SMB workgroups. Opening a computer shows its shared folders, and opening a share mounts it, asking for a password when the server needs one.
- On Linux, pinning or unpinning a folder in Flit also adds it to or removes it from the GTK and KDE sidebar bookmarks, keeping their own entries, system places and labels. Clearing Flit bookmarks leaves other programs' bookmarks alone.
- On Linux, a new Window title setting lets KDE Plasma and X11 window managers draw the title bar and frame. Where the desktop cannot, such as GNOME on Wayland or a window manager without title bars, Flit keeps its own.
- On Linux, the Scripts submenu groups Nautilus scripts, Dolphin service menus, Nemo actions and Thunar actions apart and nests Dolphin actions in their own submenus.

## Changed

- On X11, the edges of a window tiled to a screen side no longer act as resize borders, while floating windows keep all their edges.
- On X11, a drag out of Flit that lets the target ask offers Copy here, Move here and Create link here.
- On Linux, a middle click in the search window's field pastes the primary selection.
- On Linux, the palette describes Open in terminal as administrator as a root shell.

## Fixed

- On Linux, a wrong passphrase for an encrypted volume opens the passphrase dialog again instead of only showing an error.
- On Windows, the replaced flit.exe.old and flit-indexd.exe.old no longer stay in Program Files after an update installed with administrator rights.
