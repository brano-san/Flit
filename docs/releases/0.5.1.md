## Changed

- Dropping a tab on the junction between panels inserts a column along the whole separator under the cursor. The highlight shows the column that will be created, taking a third of the width from each side.
- Drop targets that would leave the panel layout as it is are no longer offered, for example moving the only tab of a panel next to itself or into itself. Holding Ctrl to copy offers them again, and the other buttons keep their places when Ctrl is pressed or released.

## Fixed

- Scrolling the mouse wheel over an empty part of an inactive panel's tab row no longer crashes Flit.
- Dragging a tab over the gap between two panels shows the drop targets of the nearest panel instead of nothing.
- While a tab is being dragged, only that tab is highlighted, not the current tab of every panel.
- The junction drop target uses the separator under the cursor, so in a split column it no longer highlights the neighbouring half.
- A tab drag ends when the window loses focus. Other mouse buttons and keyboard shortcuts no longer act on tabs during the drag.
- A session with more than 32 tabs restores all of them together with its panel layout.
- A tab dropped from another window lands in the panel under the cursor.
- With several panels, the breadcrumb scroll arrows work, the column header hover highlight appears and no longer keeps the window redrawing, and an open breadcrumb menu or tab search lights up only its own panel.
- Restored panels no longer play the new-tab animation.
- Panel separators cannot be grabbed during the short panel-closing animation, which could crash Flit.
